## Who She Is For

### Primary audience

- startup founders
- SME owners

- owner-operators

- weak pricing logic

- vague KPIs
- no structured planning
- difficulty preparing for investors or valuation

### Deeper emotional problem

- fear of numbers
- avoidance of finance
- loss of confidence in decision-making
- feeling that business growth is happening without a reliable control panel
